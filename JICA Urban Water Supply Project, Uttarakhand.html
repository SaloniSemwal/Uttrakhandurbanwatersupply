About the project

The Project for Improvement of Urban Water Supply System in Uttarakhand is supported by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA). It aims to improve water-supply facilities and provide stable services in areas with insufficient access to drinking water, including mountain and remote communities.

The project covers pumping and gravity-flow distribution systems, well-intake facilities and consulting support. Capacity development focuses on operation and maintenance and on the promotion of household connections.

Objectives

  • Improve the reliability of drinking-water supply.
  • Strengthen water-supply infrastructure and operational capacity.
  • Support better living conditions and adaptation to climate change.

How a pumping scheme works

Most schemes on the list are pumping schemes. Water is lifted from a source to a reservoir on higher ground, then flows down to households by gravity. In hill towns this lets homes below the reservoir get supply without a second pumping stage.

  1. Source: river, spring or tube well
  2. Intake or well structure
  3. Pumping station
  4. Rising main (pipe carrying water uphill)
  5. Storage reservoir on high ground
  6. Gravity distribution pipes
  7. Household connections

Frequently asked questions

Which areas does the project cover?

The 2026 list includes schemes in Haridwar, Tehri Garhwal, Almora and Pithoragarh districts. Use the search box in the Schemes section to find your town.

How do I get a household water connection?

Contact the division office listed against your scheme. The project supports the promotion of household connections.

Who funds the project?

The project is supported by a loan from the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), signed on 20 February 2024.
